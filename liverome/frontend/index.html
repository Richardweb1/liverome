What the project does

A GenLayer vault that changes behavior when the market changes.

01

Users deposit GEN

The Bradbury contract stores internal user balances and total deposits. Withdraw is internal accounting for this prototype.

02

Oracle reads the market

The contract fetches BTC 24h change and classifies it into bull, bear, sideways, or high volatility.

03

Strategy updates on-chain

Validators must agree on the deterministic result before the contract stores the new strategy.

Testing now

How to test the live contract

  1. 1
    Open GenLayer Studio

    Use the deployed contract address and your Bradbury wallet.

  2. 2
    Run deposit

    Send a small test value such as 1 GEN, then read get_total_deposits().

  3. 3
    Run rebalance

    Call rebalance() and confirm get_strategy() plus get_history().

  4. 4
    Check explorer

    Accepted transactions prove the current milestone works on Bradbury.
